The Golden Memory Trick (One Line That Never Fails)

PRE – IN – POST = position of ROOT

1.PREorder → Root before children
    NODE
    Left
    Right

2.INorder → Root in between children
    Left
    NODE
    Right

3.POSTorder → Root after children
    Left
    Right
    

🔑 One Super Simple Shortcut (Exam-proof)

Ask yourself ONE question:

Does the node depend on its children?

YES → Postorder
NO → Preorder

If it’s a BST & ordering matters → Inorder
